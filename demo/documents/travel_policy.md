# Acme Corp Travel Booking Policy

Document Type: Travel Policy
Document Reference: OPS-2026-TRV
Department: Operations
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Owner: Head of Operations

This policy covers how business travel is booked and authorised. What can
be reclaimed once travelling is set out separately in the Expense Policy
(FIN-2026-EXP).

## 1. Booking

All flights must be booked through the approved travel agent at least
14 days before departure. The approved agent is Meridian Business Travel,
reachable through the booking portal or on the out-of-hours line.

Booking outside the agent is permitted only where the agent cannot supply
the itinerary, or where a client is booking on the employee's behalf. In
either case the booking must be notified to Operations within one working
day.

Bookings made fewer than 14 days before departure require director
approval. The approval must record why the travel could not have been
booked earlier; repeated late booking by the same team is reported to the
Head of Operations quarterly.

### 1.1 Changes and Cancellations

Changes made after ticketing are charged to the originating department.
Where a change is caused by a client, the cost should be recovered
through the account manager.

Cancellations must be made through the agent so that any refundable
portion is recovered. A ticket left unused without cancellation is
treated as a loss and reported in the monthly travel review.

### 1.2 Advance Purchase

Where the itinerary is known more than 30 days ahead, the agent will
offer an advance-purchase fare. These fares are usually non-refundable.
Employees should accept the advance fare unless there is a specific
reason to expect the plan to change.

## 2. Class of Travel

Economy class is standard for all flights under 6 hours.

Premium economy is permitted on flights between 6 and 9 hours.

Business class requires director approval and is normally granted only
for flights over 9 hours, or where the employee is presenting or
negotiating within 12 hours of landing.

First class is not permitted.

### 2.1 Rail

Standard class is the default for all rail travel. First class rail is
permitted where the fare is lower than the standard class fare for the
same journey, or on journeys over 3 hours where the employee will be
working during the journey.

### 2.2 Car Hire

Car hire is permitted where public transport is impractical or where the
total cost is lower than the alternative. The vehicle category is limited
to intermediate or below.

Insurance excess waiver should be accepted. Fuel should be returned at
the level supplied; refuelling charges levied by the hire company are not
reimbursable.

## 3. Probation

Employees still on probation require additional approval before any
international travel. The approval must come from the department director
and from People Operations.

Domestic travel during probation follows the normal approval route.

## 4. International Travel

### 4.1 Authorisation

International travel requires approval from the department director
before any booking is made. The approval request must state the business
purpose, the dates, the expected cost and the client or project to which
the cost will be charged.

### 4.2 Passports and Visas

Employees are responsible for holding a valid passport. Acme Corp
reimburses the cost of a business visa and any associated appointment
fees, but not the cost of a personal passport renewal.

Visa applications should be started at least 8 weeks before travel.
Operations maintains a list of destinations where processing routinely
takes longer.

### 4.3 Travel Insurance

All employees travelling on company business are covered by the corporate
travel insurance policy. Cover is automatic once the trip is booked
through the approved agent.

Employees booking outside the agent must notify Operations so that cover
can be arranged manually. An uninsured trip is not permitted.

### 4.4 Health and Vaccinations

Required and recommended vaccinations for the destination are reimbursed
in full. Employees should consult the travel health service at least
6 weeks before departure.

### 4.5 High-Risk Destinations

Travel to a destination on the company's restricted list requires
approval from the Chief Operating Officer and a documented risk
assessment. The restricted list is maintained by Operations and reviewed
monthly.

Employees travelling to a restricted destination must complete the
pre-travel security briefing and register their itinerary with the
security desk.

## 5. Working Away From the Office

An employee travelling on business is expected to work their normal hours
where the itinerary allows. Travel time within normal working hours
counts as working time. Travel outside normal working hours does not
count as working time and does not attract overtime, except where the
employee is driving.

Where a trip requires a Sunday departure or a Saturday return, the
employee may take a compensatory half-day within the following month.

## 6. Combining Business and Personal Travel

Adding personal days to a business trip is permitted where it does not
increase the cost to the company. The employee must take annual leave for
the personal days and must be able to show that the fare would have been
the same or higher without them.

Where a personal extension increases the airfare, the employee pays the
difference. Accommodation and meals for personal days are not
reimbursable.

Travelling with a partner or family member is permitted at the employee's
own cost. The company's travel insurance does not extend to them.

## 7. Sustainability

Rail should be chosen over air for journeys under 5 hours by rail where
the total door-to-door time is comparable.

Direct flights should be preferred over connections where the fare
difference is under 15 per cent, both for emissions and for reliability.

Each department's travel emissions are reported quarterly to the board.

## 8. Ground Transport at Destination

Airport transfers by public transport or shared shuttle are preferred.
Taxis are reimbursable under the Expense Policy where public transport is
unavailable or impractical.

Ride-hailing services may be used where they are legal and licensed at
the destination.

## 9. Lost or Delayed Baggage

Reasonable costs of essential items during a baggage delay of more than
12 hours are reimbursable up to 150 GBP, net of any airline compensation.

Claims should be made to the airline first. The company reimburses the
shortfall, not the full amount.

## 10. Contacts

Booking queries: the travel portal, or travel@acme.example.
Out of hours emergencies: the agent's 24-hour line, printed on the
itinerary.
Policy questions: operations@acme.example, quoting OPS-2026-TRV.
