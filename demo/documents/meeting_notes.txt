NOTES FROM THE BENEFITS REVIEW MEETING

Held 14 November 2026, Meeting Room 3 and video link
Present: People Operations, Finance, Legal, two employee representatives
Chair: Director of People Operations
Notes taken by: People Operations coordinator

These notes are a record of discussion. They are not policy and should
not be relied on as a statement of entitlement. Where a figure is
mentioned below it is the figure under discussion, not necessarily the
figure in force.

APOLOGIES

Two apologies were received. The Head of IT and one employee
representative were unable to attend and will be sent these notes.

MATTERS ARISING FROM THE LAST MEETING

The action on benchmarking the pension contribution against the sector
has been completed. Finance circulated the benchmark pack in advance.
The action on reviewing the training budget remains open and is carried
forward again.

ANNUAL LEAVE

There was general agreement that the annual leave allowance should
continue to rise gradually over the next few years. No specific figure
was agreed and no timetable was set.

One representative asked whether the carry-over limit could be raised.
Finance noted that carry-over creates a balance sheet liability and that
raising the limit has a cost even where the leave is never taken. The
chair asked Finance to model two options for the next meeting.

A second point was raised about the timing of the carry-over deadline.
Several teams have a busy period that runs across the deadline, which
makes it hard to use carried leave in time. It was suggested that the
deadline could move, or that an exception process could be introduced.
No decision was taken.

MATERNITY AND PARTNER LEAVE

Legal confirmed that the current entitlement already exceeds the
statutory minimum. No further action was agreed.

There was a longer discussion about partner leave. One representative
argued that the current entitlement is short compared with peers and
that this affects recruitment. Finance asked for the comparison data
before committing to any change. The chair agreed to bring a paper to
the next meeting.

The point was made that take-up matters as much as entitlement. Even
where leave is available, people do not always feel able to take it. It
was agreed that People Operations would look at take-up rates by
department without identifying individuals.

SICK LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE

The attendance review trigger was discussed. Some managers feel the
current trigger is reached too easily by people managing a long-term
condition, and that the review then feels punitive even though it is
intended to be supportive.

Legal cautioned that any change needs to keep the process consistent, and
that reasonable adjustments should be handled through the adjustments
process rather than by moving the trigger.

No change was agreed. People Operations will review the guidance given
to managers instead.

PENSION

The benchmark pack showed the company contribution sitting at the median
for the sector. Two attendees felt that median was not ambitious enough
given the recruitment market.

Finance noted that any increase compounds and that the cost over five
years is significant. The chair asked for a costed paper covering a
one-point and a two-point increase.

PRIVATE MEDICAL COVER

Renewal is due in the spring. The current provider has indicated an
increase. Finance will run a market exercise.

A question was raised about extending cover to dependants at company
cost rather than employee cost. This was noted but not pursued.

REMOTE WORK ALLOWANCE

The one-off equipment allowance was felt to be working well. The monthly
contribution towards broadband and utilities was described by one
representative as token. Finance observed that the amount is set with
reference to the tax-free limit and that raising it would create a
taxable benefit, which most employees would not want.

TRAINING BUDGET

Carried forward from the last meeting and again not reached. It will be
first on the agenda next time.

ANY OTHER BUSINESS

A representative asked whether the bonus payment date could move earlier
in the year. Finance explained that the date follows the audit timetable
and cannot easily move.

A question was asked about whether policies could be published in a
single place rather than across several systems. People Operations
agreed this would be helpful and will raise it with IT.

NEXT STEPS

Finance to model the cost impact before the next review.
People Operations to bring a paper on partner leave.
People Operations to review manager guidance on attendance.
Finance to run the medical cover market exercise.
Training budget to open the next agenda.

DATE OF NEXT MEETING

February 2027, date to be confirmed by the coordinator.
