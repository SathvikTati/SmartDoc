# Acme Corp HR Policy

Document Type: HR Policy
Department: Human Resources

## 1. Leave Entitlements

### 1.1 Annual Leave

Employees accrue 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days.

### 1.2 Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to 28 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Maternity leave must be requested at least 6 weeks before the expected date.

### 1.3 Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year.

## 2. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months.
Probation may not be extended.

## 3. Notice Period

Employees must give 60 days written notice of resignation.
