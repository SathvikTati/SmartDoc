# Acme Corp HR Policy

Document Type: HR Policy
Document Reference: HR-CORE-2026
Department: Human Resources
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Review Cycle: Annual
Owner: Director of People Operations

This policy sets out the terms of employment that apply to all Acme Corp
employees in the United Kingdom and Ireland. Where a local statutory
entitlement is more generous than the terms below, the statutory
entitlement applies. Contractors and agency workers are covered by their
engagement agreement rather than by this document.

## 1. Leave Entitlements

Leave is administered through the People Portal. All leave, whether paid
or unpaid, must be recorded there even when it has been agreed verbally
with a line manager. Leave taken but not recorded is treated as
unauthorised absence under section 7.

### 1.1 Annual Leave

Employees accrue 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
exclusive of public holidays. Accrual begins on the first day of
employment and is calculated monthly in arrears.

Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days.
Carried-over leave must be taken before 31 March of the following year
or it is forfeited. Forfeited leave is not paid in lieu under any
circumstances.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement. The calculation is
the full-time entitlement multiplied by the fraction of a full week
worked. An employee working three days a week therefore receives
22 x 3 / 5 days.

Employees joining part-way through the year receive a pro-rated
entitlement for that year, calculated as the full entitlement multiplied
by the number of complete months remaining, divided by 12.

#### 1.1.1 Booking Annual Leave

Requests of five days or fewer require seven days notice. Requests of
more than five consecutive days require 21 days notice. A manager may
decline a request where business need requires it, but must give a
reason in writing and may not decline more than two consecutive requests
from the same employee.

No more than 20 per cent of any team may be on annual leave at the same
time without director approval.

#### 1.1.2 Annual Leave on Termination

On termination, accrued but untaken annual leave is paid at the
employee's daily rate. The daily rate is the annual salary divided by
260. Leave taken in excess of the accrued entitlement is recovered from
the final salary payment.

### 1.2 Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to 28 weeks of paid maternity leave.
The first 26 weeks are paid at full salary and the remaining 2 weeks at
the statutory rate.

Maternity leave must be requested at least 6 weeks before the expected
date of birth. The request must be accompanied by a MATB1 certificate or
equivalent medical evidence.

Eligibility requires 26 weeks of continuous service by the fifteenth
week before the expected week of childbirth. Employees who do not meet
the service requirement receive the statutory entitlement only.

Employees on maternity leave continue to accrue annual leave at the
normal rate, and pension contributions continue at the pre-leave salary.

### 1.3 Paternity and Partner Leave

Employees are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity or partner leave, to
be taken within 52 weeks of the birth or placement. Leave may be taken
in two separate blocks of no fewer than five working days each.

Notice of at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth is
required, though a manager may waive this where circumstances make it
impractical.

### 1.4 Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does
not carry over and is not paid in lieu.

Absence of one or two days may be self-certified through the People
Portal. Absence of three or more consecutive working days requires a
fit note from a registered medical practitioner. Absence of more than 20
working days in a rolling twelve-month period triggers a formal
attendance review under section 7.3.

Sick leave taken during a period of booked annual leave may be
reclassified as sick leave, and the annual leave returned to the
employee's balance, provided a fit note is supplied.

### 1.5 Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave following the
death of an immediate family member, and 2 days following the death of a
member of the extended family. Immediate family means a spouse, partner,
child, parent or sibling.

Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of the
department director.

### 1.6 Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted after two years of
continuous service. Requests require director approval and must be made
at least 8 weeks in advance. Continuity of service is preserved, but
annual leave does not accrue during unpaid leave.

## 2. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. Probation may not be
extended.

During probation the notice period is 1 week on either side. On
successful completion, the notice terms in section 3 apply.

A probation review meeting is held at the six-week point and again in
the final week. Both meetings are recorded in the People Portal. An
employee who does not pass probation is notified in writing with reasons
within five working days of the final review.

Employees on probation are not eligible for the discretionary bonus
scheme, the training budget described in section 5.2, or unpaid leave
under section 1.6.

## 3. Notice Periods

Employees must give 60 days written notice of resignation. Acme Corp
gives the same notice on termination other than for gross misconduct.

Notice must be given in writing to the employee's line manager and to
People Operations. Notice given verbally has no effect.

Acme Corp may require an employee to work all, part, or none of the
notice period. Where an employee is placed on garden leave they remain
employed, continue to receive full salary and benefits, and remain bound
by the confidentiality terms of their contract.

Payment in lieu of notice is at the discretion of the company and is
calculated as the basic salary that would have been paid during the
notice period, excluding bonus and allowances.

## 4. Working Hours and Flexibility

### 4.1 Standard Hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours, worked between 09:00 and 17:30
with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Core hours, during which all
employees are expected to be contactable, are 10:00 to 16:00.

### 4.2 Flexible Working

Any employee may request a flexible working arrangement from their first
day. Requests are considered against business need and answered within
28 days. A refusal must state which of the statutory business grounds
applies.

Approved arrangements are reviewed after 6 months and then annually.

### 4.3 Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. Fully
remote arrangements require director approval and a home workstation
assessment.

Employees working remotely must comply with the security requirements in
the Information Security Policy, in particular the device and network
controls. Working from outside the United Kingdom or Ireland for more
than 10 consecutive working days requires prior approval from People
Operations for tax and immigration reasons.

## 5. Performance and Development

### 5.1 Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews are held twice a year, in March and
September. Each review records objectives for the following period, an
assessment against the previous objectives, and a development plan.

Ratings are on a five-point scale: Outstanding, Exceeds, Meets, Partially
Meets, Does Not Meet. A rating of Does Not Meet triggers a performance
improvement plan under section 7.4.

### 5.2 Training Budget

Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 GBP. The budget
covers courses, conferences, certifications and books. It does not cover
hardware, which is provided under the IT Acceptable Use Policy.

Unused training budget does not carry over. Expenditure above 500 GBP in
a single item requires manager approval in advance.

### 5.3 Promotion

Promotion decisions are made in a calibration meeting held after each
September review cycle. A promotion requires a rating of Exceeds or
above in the two most recent reviews, and a written case from the line
manager.

## 6. Pay and Benefits

### 6.1 Salary Review

Salaries are reviewed annually with effect from 1 April. The review
considers performance rating, position within the salary band, and
market data.

### 6.2 Discretionary Bonus

The bonus scheme pays up to 10 per cent of basic salary, determined by
company performance and individual rating. Bonuses are paid in the April
payroll and require the employee to be in post and not under notice on
the payment date.

### 6.3 Pension

Acme Corp contributes 6 per cent of basic salary to the workplace
pension where the employee contributes 4 per cent or more. Employees are
enrolled automatically after 3 months of service and may opt out at any
time.

### 6.4 Private Medical Cover

Employees are enrolled in the company medical scheme after completing
probation. Cover extends to a spouse or partner at the employee's own
cost through payroll deduction.

## 7. Conduct and Absence Management

### 7.1 Standards of Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with
respect, and comply with all company policies. The full code of conduct
is set out in the Employee Handbook.

### 7.2 Unauthorised Absence

Absence that is neither booked as leave nor reported as sickness is
unauthorised. Unauthorised absence is unpaid and may be treated as a
disciplinary matter.

### 7.3 Attendance Review

An attendance review is triggered by more than 20 working days of
sickness absence in a rolling twelve-month period, or by four or more
separate absence episodes in the same period. The review is supportive
rather than punitive in the first instance and considers whether a
workplace adjustment would help.

### 7.4 Performance Improvement

A performance improvement plan runs for 8 weeks, with fortnightly
review meetings and written objectives. Failure to meet the objectives
may result in dismissal following the disciplinary procedure in the
Employee Handbook.

## 8. Grievances

An employee who wishes to raise a grievance should first discuss it
informally with their line manager. Where that is not appropriate, or
where the informal route does not resolve the matter, a formal written
grievance may be submitted to People Operations.

The formal procedure is set out in full in the Employee Handbook.

## 9. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations and approved by the
board. Material changes are communicated to all employees at least 30
days before they take effect. Questions about this policy should be
directed to people@acme.example.
