# Acme Corp IT Acceptable Use Policy

Document Type: IT Policy
Document Reference: IT-AUP-204
Department: Information Technology
Effective Date: 1 March 2026
Owner: Head of IT

This policy sets out how Acme Corp equipment, accounts and networks may
be used. It complements the Information Security Policy, which sets the
mandatory controls; this document covers day-to-day use.

Acceptance of this policy is recorded during onboarding and again
annually. Continued use of company systems constitutes acceptance.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every device that connects to a company system,
whether owned by Acme Corp or by the employee, and to every account
issued in the company's name. It applies equally in the office, at home,
and while travelling.

## 2. Company Devices

### 2.1 Issue and Return

Each employee is issued a laptop appropriate to their role. The standard
refresh cycle is 3 years. Devices are returned on the last working day
of employment; a device not returned within 14 days is charged at its
book value.

Peripherals — monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset — are issued on request
and do not count against the training budget or the remote work
allowance.

### 2.2 Maintenance

Employees must install operating system updates within 14 days of
release, and security patches within 7 days. Devices that fall more than
30 days behind are blocked from the network automatically until they are
patched.

Employees must not disable endpoint protection, full-disk encryption, or
the mobile device management agent.

### 2.3 Loss and Theft

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk
immediately and in any case within 4 hours of discovery. The device will
be wiped remotely.

Loss through negligence — leaving a laptop visible in a parked car, for
example — may be handled under the disciplinary procedure. The first
instance of ordinary bad luck is not.

## 3. Bring Your Own Device

Personal devices may access company email and calendar only, and only
with the mobile device management profile installed. Personal devices may
not access source code, customer data, or the finance systems.

Installing the management profile allows Acme Corp to wipe company data
from the device. It does not allow the company to read personal
messages, photographs, or browsing history.

An employee who does not wish to install the profile is not required to,
and will be issued a company phone instead where the role needs one.

## 4. Software

### 4.1 Approved Software

Software from the self-service catalogue may be installed without
approval. Anything else requires a request to the IT service desk, which
checks the licence terms and the security posture of the vendor.

Installing unlicensed software is prohibited. Where a licence is needed,
the company buys it.

### 4.2 Open Source

Open-source components may be used in company products subject to the
licence review process maintained by Legal. Copyleft licences require
review before use in any distributed product.

### 4.3 Browser Extensions

Browser extensions have broad access to everything displayed in the
browser. Only extensions from the approved list may be installed.

## 5. Artificial Intelligence Tools

### 5.1 Approved Tools

The approved AI tools are listed in the self-service catalogue. Only
tools on that list may be used with company information.

### 5.2 What May Not Be Entered

Customer personal data, unreleased financial results, source code from
private repositories, credentials, and anything classified Confidential
or above under the Information Security Policy must not be entered into
any AI tool, approved or otherwise, unless the tool is running under a
company agreement that excludes training on submitted data.

### 5.3 Accountability

Output from an AI tool is the responsibility of the employee who used it.
Code must be reviewed before merge, and text must be checked for accuracy
before it is sent to a customer.

## 6. Email and Messaging

Company email is for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted
provided it is reasonable and does not interfere with work.

Employees must not use company email to register for personal services,
nor auto-forward company email to a personal account.

Suspected phishing should be reported through the Report Phishing button
rather than deleted. Reporting is never penalised, even where the message
turns out to be legitimate.

## 7. Internet Use

Reasonable personal use of the internet is permitted during breaks.

The following are prohibited: accessing or distributing unlawful
material; circumventing the content filter; running peer-to-peer file
sharing; using company bandwidth for cryptocurrency mining; and
attempting to access systems the employee is not authorised to use.

## 8. Passwords and Access

Passwords must meet the requirements in the Information Security Policy.
Every employee must use the company password manager; passwords must not
be stored in browsers, spreadsheets, or notes.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts.
Employees must not approve an authentication prompt they did not
initiate, and must report any unexpected prompt to the security desk.

Accounts must not be shared. Where a shared function is needed, IT will
provision a service account with an audited credential.

## 9. Data Handling

### 9.1 Storage

Company data must be stored in approved systems. Storing company data on
personal cloud accounts, personal email, or unencrypted removable media
is prohibited.

### 9.2 Data Retention

Data is retained according to the Data Retention Schedule. Employees
should not keep local copies of records beyond the period stated there,
and should not delete records that are within their retention period or
subject to a legal hold.

### 9.3 Transfer

Files sent outside the company must go through the approved file transfer
service where they contain anything classified Internal or above.

## 10. Monitoring

Acme Corp monitors network traffic, email metadata, and system access
logs for security and capacity purposes. Monitoring is proportionate and
is not used for routine performance management.

Content of communications is examined only where there is a specific and
documented reason, approved by the Head of IT and by Legal.

Logs are retained according to the Data Retention Schedule.

## 11. Leaving the Company

On the last working day, accounts are disabled, devices are returned, and
access badges are surrendered. Data belonging to the company must not be
copied, forwarded, or retained.

Personal files stored on a company device should be removed before the
device is returned. IT cannot recover them afterwards.

## 12. Breaches

A breach of this policy may be handled under the disciplinary procedure.
Serious breaches — deliberately disabling security controls, exfiltrating
company data, or accessing systems without authorisation — are gross
misconduct.

Reporting your own mistake promptly is treated as a mitigating factor.
