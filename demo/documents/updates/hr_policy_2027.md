# Acme Corp HR Policy

Document Type: HR Policy
Document Reference: HR-CORE-2027
Department: Human Resources
Effective Date: 1 January 2027
Supersedes: HR-CORE-2026
Review Cycle: Annual
Owner: Director of People Operations

This revision follows the November 2026 benefits review. The changes are
summarised in section 0; everything not listed there is unchanged from
HR-CORE-2026.

## 0. Summary of Changes

Annual leave rises from 22 days to 25 days.

The carry-over limit rises from 10 days to 12 days.

Sick leave rises from 12 days to 15 days.

The notice period changes from 60 days to 90 days for both parties.

Paternity and partner leave rises from 4 weeks to 6 weeks.

The training budget rises from 1,500 GBP to 2,000 GBP.

Nothing else has changed. Probation, working hours, remote work, the
pension contribution and the bonus scheme are all as they were.

## 1. Leave Entitlements

### 1.1 Annual Leave

Employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
exclusive of public holidays. Accrual begins on the first day of
employment and is calculated monthly in arrears.

Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 12 days.
Carried-over leave must be taken before 31 March of the following year
or it is forfeited. Forfeited leave is not paid in lieu.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement, calculated as the
full-time entitlement multiplied by the fraction of a full week worked.

Employees joining part-way through the year receive a pro-rated
entitlement for that year.

### 1.2 Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to 28 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Maternity leave must be requested at least 6 weeks before the expected
date of birth.

This entitlement is unchanged from the previous revision.

### 1.3 Paternity and Partner Leave

Employees are entitled to 6 weeks of paid paternity or partner leave, to
be taken within 52 weeks of the birth or placement.

### 1.4 Sick Leave

Employees receive 15 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does
not carry over and is not paid in lieu.

Absence of one or two days may be self-certified. Absence of three or
more consecutive working days requires a fit note.

## 2. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. Probation may not be
extended.

This section is unchanged from the previous revision.

## 3. Notice Periods

Employees must give 90 days written notice of resignation. Acme Corp
gives the same notice on termination other than for gross misconduct.

The increase from 60 days reflects the difficulty of replacing
specialist roles. It applies to all employees from the effective date,
including those already in post.

## 4. Development

### 4.1 Training Budget

Each employee has an annual training budget of 2,000 GBP. The budget
covers courses, conferences, certifications and books.

Unused training budget does not carry over. Expenditure above 500 GBP in
a single item requires manager approval in advance.

## 5. Everything Else

All other terms — working hours, flexible working, remote work,
performance reviews, promotion, salary review, the discretionary bonus,
pension, private medical cover, conduct, absence management and
grievances — are unchanged from HR-CORE-2026 and are not restated here.

Refer to the previous revision for their full text.
