ACME CORP NEW STARTER ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

Reference: HR-ONB-2026
For: line managers and new starters
Maintained by: People Operations

Work through this checklist in order. Items marked MANDATORY block system
access until they are complete. Everything else can follow in the first
fortnight.

BEFORE THE FIRST DAY

Manager confirms the start date with People Operations at least 10
working days ahead. A later confirmation risks the laptop not arriving in
time.

Manager raises the equipment request in the IT portal, choosing the
laptop specification appropriate to the role. Standard build for most
roles, developer build where the role writes code, and the high-memory
build for design and data work.

Manager nominates a buddy. The buddy is not the line manager and is
usually someone who joined within the last year.

Manager drafts a 30-60-90 day plan. This does not need to be detailed but
it should say what good looks like at each point.

People Operations sends the contract and the new starter pack. The pack
includes the HR Policy, the Information Security Policy, the IT
Acceptable Use Policy and the Expense Policy.

New starter returns the signed contract. MANDATORY.

New starter supplies right to work documentation. MANDATORY. Originals
must be seen, in person or over video with the original held to camera.

New starter supplies bank details and tax information through the People
Portal. MANDATORY for the first payroll run; a late submission means
payment in the following month.

DAY ONE

Collect the laptop and access badge from reception, or unbox the laptop
delivered to home.

Complete first login and enrol in multi-factor authentication. MANDATORY.
Access to every other system depends on this.

Install the password manager and move any temporary credentials into it.
MANDATORY.

Read and accept the IT Acceptable Use Policy in the compliance portal.
MANDATORY.

Read and accept the Information Security Policy in the compliance portal.
MANDATORY.

Meet the line manager for a first one-to-one. Cover the 30-60-90 plan,
the working pattern, and how the team communicates.

Meet the buddy. No agenda required.

Photograph for the internal directory, if the new starter is happy to
have one.

FIRST WEEK

Security awareness training. MANDATORY within 5 working days. The module
takes about 40 minutes and ends with a short assessment.

Data protection training. MANDATORY within 5 working days.

Set up the development environment, for engineering roles. The setup
guide is in the engineering handbook.

Attend the company introduction session, held every Tuesday morning.

Book a first meeting with each of the immediate team.

Confirm the working pattern with the line manager and record it in the
People Portal, including any remote days.

Read the Travel Booking Policy and the Expense Policy if the role
involves travel.

FIRST MONTH

Complete the code of conduct module. MANDATORY within 30 days.

Complete the anti-bribery module. MANDATORY within 30 days for anyone in
a client-facing or purchasing role.

Enrol in the pension scheme. Automatic enrolment happens after 3 months
of service, but a new starter may opt in earlier.

Attend a session with People Operations covering leave booking,
performance reviews and the benefits package.

First deliverable, as set out in the 30-60-90 plan.

PROBATION

New starters serve a probation period of 3 months. The terms are set out
in the HR Policy and are not repeated here.

A probation review meeting is held at the six-week point. The manager
books this at the start of employment so that it is in the calendar and
does not slip.

The final probation review is held in the last week of the third month.
The outcome is recorded in the People Portal within five working days.

Note that employees on probation are not eligible for the discretionary
bonus scheme or for unpaid leave, and that international travel during
probation needs additional approval. Managers should flag this early
where a role involves travel.

MANAGER CHECKLIST SUMMARY

Ten working days before: confirm start date, raise equipment request,
nominate buddy, draft the 30-60-90 plan.

Day one: one-to-one, introduce the buddy, confirm access is working.

Week one: check the mandatory training is booked, confirm the working
pattern is recorded.

Week six: hold the first probation review.

Month three: hold the final probation review and record the outcome.

COMMON PROBLEMS

Laptop not arriving in time. Usually caused by a late start date
confirmation. Escalate to the IT service desk with the request number.

Payroll missed. Usually caused by bank details or tax information not
being submitted before payroll closes on the 20th.

Access to a system missing. Access is role-based; ask the manager to
check that the correct role was requested rather than raising a ticket
for each system individually.

Training overdue. Reminders are automatic at 3, 5 and 10 days. After
that the compliance report goes to the department director.
