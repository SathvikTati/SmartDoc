# Acme Corp Expense Policy

Document Type: Expense Policy
Department: Finance

## 1. Travel Expenses

### 1.1 Meals

Employees may expense meals during business travel up to 60 USD per day.

### 1.2 Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at 250 USD per night in major cities
and 150 USD per night elsewhere.

## 2. Reimbursement

Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD.
Expense reports must be filed within 30 days of the trip ending.
Claims are processed under reference code FIN-2026-EXP.

## 3. Non-Reimbursable Items

Personal entertainment, minibar charges and flight upgrades are not
reimbursable under any circumstances.
